Remittance Stack

You’re on a work visa, a time zone away. Stack the transfers — rent for family, a celebration at home, school fees — and clear them in matching bundles before the window closes. When the money lands, the Sunday video call feels lighter.

Tap a column, or use the arrow keys and Space, to drop the next transfer. Groups of three or more of the same colour send as one bundle. Hit the monthly goal to win. If the queue fills up, the window closes.
