LinkedIn Network Insights

See who left your connections, who your real friends are, who is ignoring your messages, and who actually writes back. This page never opens LinkedIn for you, never scrapes anything, and never uploads your file. You drop in the official data archive LinkedIn already lets you download.

Get your official export first

The fastest path is LinkedIn’s own download page. This button opens it in a new tab. This tool does not log in, does not talk to LinkedIn, and does not read your account.

Get my LinkedIn data

  1. 1

    Request the larger archive

    Choose Download larger data archive, or tick at least Connections and Messages. Then tap Request archive. Invitations, Reactions and Comments are useful extras if they are offered.

  2. 2

    Wait for the email

    A first zip often arrives in about 10 minutes (Connections, Invitations and Messages are usually in it). The full archive can take up to 24 hours. The download link stays live for 72 hours.

  3. 3

    Drop the zip here

    Come back when the email arrives, download the zip, and drop it on the area below — or tap it and pick the file. Individual CSV files work too.

You can close this tab while you wait. Come back when the email arrives — your file is read only on this device, then forgotten when you leave.

Upload your export

Drop your LinkedIn zip here

Or tap to upload from your phone or computer. Zips and CSV files are both fine. Nothing is sent anywhere.
