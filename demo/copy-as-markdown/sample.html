Watering Schedules

Plants need less water than most people think. Read the soil guide first, then set a schedule with garden.schedule(). Hidden promo text.

How often to water

Water deeply but rarely. A quick test:
push a finger 2 cm into the soil.

  1. Check the soil in the morning.
  2. Water at the base, not the leaves.
    • Tomatoes: every 2 days
    • Succulents: every 2 weeks
  3. Skip a day after rain.

Set it up in code

const plan = garden.schedule({
  plant: "tomato",
  everyDays: 2,
});

Overwatering kills more houseplants than underwatering.
