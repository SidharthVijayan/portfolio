Hydration and crumb structure in 100% rye sourdough loaves

Maya Fernandes, Daniel Okafor and Sara Lindqvist · Published 15 September 2026

Abstract. Demo article for testing. We baked 36 all-rye loaves at five dough hydrations (70% to 90%) and scored crumb gumminess, cracking and moisture after 24 hours. Loaves at 80% hydration had the most even crumb and the fewest surface cracks.

1. Method

Each loaf used the same dark rye flour, a 20% starter and 2% salt. Doughs were proofed until the first surface cracks appeared and baked in lidded tins.

2. Results

HydrationEven crumbCracks
70%2 / 65 / 6

90%3 / 60 / 6

Above 85% the crumb stayed gummy for longer, even after a full day of cooling.
