Why City Trees Matter

Draft 2 · Word limit: 150 · Class 11 English

On a hot afternoon, the difference between a street with trees and a street without them is easy to feel. Shade can make the pavement several degrees cooler, and people choose to walk where the branches meet overhead.

Trees also clean the air we breathe. Their leaves trap dust and absorb some of the gases that traffic leaves behind. A single mature tree will not fix a polluted road, but a row of them makes a real difference over the years.

Finally, trees give a neighbourhood its character. Children remember the tree they climbed, and older residents measure time by how tall the saplings have grown. When we plant a tree, we are writing a note to the people who will live here after us.
